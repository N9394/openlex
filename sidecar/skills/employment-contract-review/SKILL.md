---
name: employment-contract-review
title: Employment contract review
description: Check an employment contract for compliance with mandatory employment law and for clauses that are unfavourable to the client (employer or employee).
triggers: [employment contract, arbeitsvertrag, dienstvertrag, pracovná zmluva, pracovní smlouva, contract de muncă, offer letter]
output: suggestions
---
1. Identify the client side and the jurisdiction; note any applicable collective agreement the contract names.
2. Check: role and place of work, working time and overtime pay, remuneration and bonus (discretion, clawback), holiday, probation, notice periods and termination dates, non-compete (scope, duration, compensation, statutory limits), confidentiality and IP assignment, expenses, data protection notice, choice of law.
3. search_law for every restrictive covenant and notice period before judging it: these are the clauses most often void under mandatory law.
4. Distinguish "void under mandatory law" from "unfavourable but valid".

Output: suggestion cards and a two-line summary of the compliance issues.
