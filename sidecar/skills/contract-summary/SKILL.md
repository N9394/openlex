triggers: [summary, summarise, summarize, zusammenfassung, zusammenfassen, fasse, kurz zusammen, key terms, term sheet, overview, zhrnutie, shrnutí, rezumat]

1. Read the document map and the operative clauses.
2. Produce a key-terms sheet: parties, subject matter, term and termination, price and payment, main obligations of each side, liability and caps, IP and confidentiality, governing law and forum, unusual clauses.
3. Every line cites the clause number. Copy amounts, dates and deadlines exactly as written; never round or convert.
4. Write in the language the lawyer used for the request.

Output: plain text, one line per term, no suggestions.
